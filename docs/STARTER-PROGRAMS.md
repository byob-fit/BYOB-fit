# BYOB-fit starter programs (O-8, approved Sep 28, 2026, D-037)

Status: drafted by Claude, APPROVED by the owner on Sep 28, 2026 without changes (D-037). Exercise how-to text remains DEFAULT until the demo clips (O-6) exist. Full gym only; a home version is a later feature (stated by the owner, Sep 28, 2026). Placeholder `startDate` (2026-01-04) is replaced by onboarding. Loads start empty.

## Design rules and where they come from

| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| Every major muscle group trained at least twice a week | ACSM Position Stand 2026 (press release, Mar 17, 2026): training all major muscle groups at least twice a week matters most. VERIFIED this session, single source (the full paper is in Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise) |
| About 10 weekly sets per muscle group for muscle growth, for the intermediate and experienced programs | Same source: higher weekly volume, about 10 sets per muscle group |
| Heavier compound work (5 to 8 reps, longer rest) in the intermediate and experienced programs | Same source: heavier loads, around 80% of 1RM, for 2 to 3 sets, for strength |
| Beginner: 8 to 12 reps, full body, 3 days a week | ACSM 2009 position stand (novice: 8 to 12 RM, 2 to 3 days a week). Superseded by the 2026 stand; used only for the beginner rep range. VERIFIED this session |
| No training to failure, no complex periodization | 2026 stand: failure, equipment type and complex periodization did not consistently change outcomes for healthy adults |
| Large, multi-joint exercises before small, single-joint ones | ACSM 2009 sequencing recommendation. VERIFIED this session |
| Session length | MODELED: sets × (about 40 s of work + prescribed rest) + 1 min per exercise + warm-up, rounded to 5 min |

## Coverage matrix (built before any conclusion; primary muscles only)

| Program | Days | Minutes per session | Weekly sets: chest / back / shoulders / legs / glutes / arms (direct) / core | Days a week each group is trained |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3-day full body (beginner, 8 weeks) | Mon Full body A, Wed Full body B, Fri Full body A | 45, 45, 45 | 8 / 9 / 7 / 18 / 18 / 0 / 9 | 3 / 3 / 3 / 3 / 3 / 0 / 3 (same order) |
| 4-day upper/lower (intermediate, 10 weeks) | Mon Upper A, Tue Lower A, Thu Upper B, Fri Lower B | 55, 55, 60, 50 | 11 / 20 / 13 / 31 / 19 / 6 / 6 | 2 / 3 / 2 / 2 / 2 / 2 / 2 (same order) |
| 5-day split (experienced, 12 weeks) | Mon Push, Tue Pull, Wed Legs, Fri Upper, Sat Lower | 60, 55, 55, 60, 55 | 17 / 23 / 23 / 37 / 23 / 10 / 6 | 2 / 2 / 3 / 3 / 3 / 3 / 2 (same order) |

Reading the matrix: every major group is trained at least twice a week in all three programs; in the 3-day program the arms get no direct work and are trained through presses and rows. The beginner program sits below 10 sets on purpose (ACSM 2009 starts novices at 1 to 3 sets). "Legs" lumps quads, hamstrings and calves, which is why it looks high. Arms count direct work only; presses and rows also train them.

## Notes for the build

Session lengths are modeled and shorter than the placeholder labels in the design (45 / 60 / 75 min); onboarding shows the computed values. Onboarding suggests the 3-day program to users who answer New and the 4-day program to users who answer Experienced.

## Not included, on purpose

No progression rules yet (that is O-10, before Phase 10). No deloads: the 2026 stand found complex periodization did not consistently change outcomes, and the weekly AI update can add one. No home version (later feature).
